BeatTheLine uses an NYU-inspired deep violet identity with lavender surfaces and a restrained warm-gold accent. This is palette inspiration, not an official NYU affiliation.

**Why:** The product owner asked for violet so the app resembles NYU.

**How to apply:** Reuse the semantic palette across student and staff screens; do not add university marks or imply an official relationship unless the product owner provides them.
