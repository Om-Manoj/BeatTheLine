name: BeatTheLine dining rules
description: Product constraints for student pickup ordering.
---

Checkout collects the student's name and a mock payment-method choice; no payment is charged and payment remains due at pickup. The prototype offers meal swipes (AED 33.60 each, whole swipes only), Flex dirhams, and Campus dirhams. Whole-swipe change/rollover policy is unspecified. NetID is a visual sign-in preview only: do not collect credentials or claim an account is linked until official university SSO is configured. Pickup times run every 10 minutes from 11:30 AM through 1:30 PM, with a maximum of five orders per slot.

**Why:** These are user-stated dining rules. The user specifically requested a theoretical payment-choice and NetID-linking preview without connecting to campus systems.

**How to apply:** Keep payment and NetID flows clearly labeled as mockups until the user supplies an official campus identity/payment integration. Never collect NetID passwords. Preserve the whole-swipe denomination and pickup capacity rules.
