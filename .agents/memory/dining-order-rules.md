---
name: BeatTheLine dining rules
description: Product constraints for student pickup ordering.
---

For the first version, checkout asks only for the student's name and payment is due at pickup. Pickup times run every 10 minutes from 11:30 AM through 1:30 PM, with a maximum of five orders per slot.

**Why:** These are the user-stated operating rules for the university dining hall.

**How to apply:** Preserve them when extending checkout, pickup scheduling, or order fulfillment.
